Soupyy Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026 · Soupyy 2.0 for Android

In short: everything you create in Soupyy stays on your phone. The developer has no server and never receives your data.

Your data stays on your device

Soupyy stores your account, recipes, photos, cookbooks, follows, likes and comments locally on your phone only. We cannot see, access or share any of it. The one exception is signing in with Google, described below.

What is stored on your phone

Your email address and, if you use a password, a salted scrypt hash of it (never the password itself); your display name, handle, bio and profile photos; the recipes, photos, cookbooks and activity you create; and your theme choice.

Signing in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name and email address with Soupyy on your phone to create or open your local account. This happens between your phone and Google; Soupyy has no server to receive it. See Google's own privacy policy for Google's side.

Camera and photos

Soupyy uses the camera app or the system photo picker only when you add a photo. Photos you choose are copied into the app's private storage on your phone and are never sent anywhere.

Backups

Settings → Export My Data saves a backup file wherever you choose. It contains your recipes, photos, cookbooks and comments, and stays under your control. Android's own device backup may also include the app's data in your Google account backup, as for any app.

No tracking, analytics or ads

Soupyy contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it has no internet permission of its own.

Deleting your data

Delete your account and everything it created at any time from Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data. Because nothing is stored on a server, there is nothing else to request.

Children

Soupyy is intended for adults and does not knowingly store information from children.
