Soupyy Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026 · Soupyy for Android

In short: from version 2.1, Soupyy keeps your account and recipes in the cloud so they survive reinstalls and move with you to a new phone. Public recipes and your profile can be seen by other Soupyy users. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you. Soupyy 2.0 kept everything on your phone only.

What we store

To run your account we store, with our hosting provider Supabase (servers in India, ap-south-1):

  • Your email address. Passwords are handled by Supabase's sign-in service and stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text.
  • Your profile: display name, handle, bio, profile photo and cover photo.
  • What you create: recipes (and their earlier versions), recipe photos, cookbooks, saved recipes, likes, follows, comments, blocks and reports.
  • Notifications sent to you, such as likes, comments and new followers.

Your phone also keeps a copy of this data so the app can open offline, plus your theme choice and drafts.

Who can see it

Public recipes and their photos, your profile, your comments, likes and follows can be seen by other Soupyy users. Private recipes, your saved recipes, cookbooks, blocks, reports and notifications are visible only to you. Photo files are stored at web addresses that are hard to guess but are not password protected. Recipes from cooks you block are hidden from you, and blocked cooks cannot follow you, like or comment on your recipes.

Signing in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name and email address with Soupyy to create or sign in to your account. See Google's own privacy policy for Google's side.

Camera and photos

Soupyy uses the camera app or the system photo picker only when you add a photo. Only the photos you choose are uploaded.

Backups

Settings → Export My Data saves a file copy of your recipes, photos, cookbooks and comments wherever you choose. It stays under your control.

No tracking, analytics or ads

Soupyy contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your data is never sold or shared for marketing. It is used only to run the app.

Deleting your data

Delete your account at any time from Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, recipes, photos, cookbooks, comments, likes, follows and notifications from our servers straight away. If you can't open the app, email us from your account's address and we will delete it for you.

Soupyy 2.0

Version 2.0 had no server: everything stayed on your phone, and uninstalling removed it. When you update to 2.1 and sign in, the app offers to upload your 2.0 recipes and cookbooks into your cloud account; you can skip this.

Children

Soupyy is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from children.
